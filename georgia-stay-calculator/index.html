How does Georgia count the year?

The 365 days run from the day you enter. Leaving and coming back starts a new count, which is why the rule is usually described as a year per entry rather than days in a window.

Do frequent border runs cause trouble?

They can. A new entry is the border officer's decision, and someone who seems to live in Georgia on back-to-back visa-free stays may be asked about it. For a longer life there, a residence permit is the way in.

Can I work on a visa-free stay?

Staying visa-free is not the same as being allowed to work. Georgia has been tightening its rules for foreigners who work there, so check what applies to you before you take a job.

What about tax?

Georgia makes you tax resident after 183 days in any continuous twelve months ending in the tax year. The 183-day calculator counts that.
