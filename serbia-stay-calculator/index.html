Does leaving Serbia reset the 30 days?

On paper, yes: for Russian citizens the 30 days run from each entry. In practice, in 2026 Serbian border and police officers have been reported to look at the total time spent in the country, and a court in Subotica treated repeated 30-day stays as one when they added up to more than 90 in 180. The strict preset counts that way. A residence permit is the safe way to stay longer.

How does the 90-in-180 rule work in Serbia?

Like Schengen's, but on its own: on any day in Serbia, the 180 days ending that day may hold at most 90 in Serbia. Days in the Schengen area do not count towards it.

Do I need to register?

Yes. Foreigners register their address with the police within 24 hours of arrival, the 'white card'; a hotel does it for its guests.

What about tax?

Serbia treats 183 days in any twelve months as a sign of tax residency. The 183-day calculator counts that.

Rules as checked on 5 November 2026. They change — the country's foreign ministry or embassy has the final word, and this page is a calculator, not legal advice.
